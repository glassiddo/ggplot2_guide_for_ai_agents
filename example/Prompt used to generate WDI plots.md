# Prompt Used to Generate WDI Plots

Using the WDI Clean.dta dataset, create plots while strictly following the guide in short_ggplot_guide.md. The dataset is based on World Bank World Development Indicators and each row is a country-year. There are some missing values. Variables refer to aid, gdp, population, gdp by sectors, employment by sector.

## Guiding questions to explore

1. How did urban population change over time as averages for each region name?
2. How did the share of employed population in agriculture change over time as average for each region name? And in services?
3. While only showing Sub-Saharan African countries, highlight the trajectories of EmpltoPopRatio in Tanzania and Ghana over time.
4. In 2010, show comparisons including on a map for share of employment in agriculture across Sub-Saharan Africa.
5. What are the leading countries in the world in terms of urb_pop? And what are the ones with the lowest values? Show it over time.
6. Are urban population and gdp_pc correlated? Create two plots of the same kind, but one uses different colors for different regions, and one highlights just Sub-Saharan Africa.
7. Show the sum of different components of GDP (the various share_va_...) in the world over time — one as shares and one as absolute sum (stacked area).

Put the plots in a relevant subfolder.
